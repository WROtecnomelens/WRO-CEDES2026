# WRO-CEDES2026
This account will be used for academic purposes to develop and manage our WRO project, allowing us to organize code, document progress, and collaborate efficiently among team members, ensuring clear tracking of progress and facilitating future improvements to the project in an orderly and secure manner.

Overview
Our team participates in the Future Engineers category of the WRO with the objective of designing and building an autonomous vehicle capable of moving efficiently, detecting its environment, and reacting in real time.

The robot operates as a self-driving vehicle that can navigate a track autonomously, detect obstacles, and make decisions in real time. It addresses the challenge of autonomous navigation, demonstrating how vehicles can move safely without human intervention. This is important because autonomous driving technology has the potential to improve road safety, reduce human error, and optimize transportation systems.

The project is based on the integration of sensors, artificial intelligence, and electronic control to simulate the behavior of a real autonomous car.
